1. Today's composer

Unchanged, to compare against: the settings row ends with the env button.

2. The composer with repos

New: a repos button next to env (with a count) opens a panel under the row: one row per repo, its clone URL and an optional ref (a branch, tag or commit; empty is the default branch), add and remove.

3. Errors, inline

New: a bad row is flagged on its field before anything is sent, as env's are (the server's rules: a URL or ref starting with -, a ref with spaces, a ref without a URL, two repos that would land in the same folder); Start stays disabled until they are fixed.

4. Folded

New: as with env, the panel folds from its button (▸, and the count stays), and the panel's group folds on its own (▸ with a count).

5. The session page

New: Details gets a repos row, per repo its URL, its ref (or "default branch"), its folder in the workplace and the session's branch; URL, folder and branch copy on a click, as the id does.
